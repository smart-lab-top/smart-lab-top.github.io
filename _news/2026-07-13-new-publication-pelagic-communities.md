We are pleased to announce the publication of our latest research paper in **Ecology and Evolution**.

### [The Spatiotemporal Patterns and Environmental Effects of Pelagic Communities in the Northwest Pacific Ocean Based on Sound Scattering Layer Descriptors](https://doi.org/10.1002/ece3.73947)

**Authors:** Xue, M., & Tong, Jianfeng (Corresponding Author)

#### Summary

This study explores the complex spatiotemporal dynamics of pelagic communities in the Northwest Pacific Ocean. By leveraging **Sound Scattering Layer (SSL) descriptors**, we provide new insights into how environmental factors influence the distribution and behavior of marine organisms. Our findings contribute to a deeper understanding of the ecological structure of the Northwest Pacific and the efficacy of acoustic descriptors in monitoring marine biodiversity.

**Key Highlights:**

- Application of SSL descriptors to characterize pelagic community patterns.
- Analysis of environmental drivers affecting community distribution in the NW Pacific.
- Advancement in acoustic-based ecological monitoring.
